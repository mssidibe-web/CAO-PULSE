# C.A.O public diagnostic - evidence shaping the demo

Access/review date: 30 September 2026.

## What is demonstrated publicly
1. C.A.O presents itself as an independent accounting firm and a trusted partner for management, optimization and regulatory compliance; its public positioning explicitly mentions transformation, performance, innovation and nearly 20 years of work with the State, companies and technical/financial partners. Source: https://cabinetcao.com/a-propos/
2. A World Bank procurement award published in 2026 for the PRAPS 2-ML external audit identifies Cabinet C.A.O as the awarded firm, with technical score 97, financial score 100 and combined score 97.6. This is strong public evidence of competitive technical and commercial execution on a major audit procurement. Source observed via World Bank procurement notice syndication: https://freetender.net/tender/audit-externe-du-praps-2-ml-exercices-2025-2026-et-2027-op00462860 . Before external publication, re-check the underlying World Bank STEP record.
3. LinkedIn publicly describes C.A.O as accounting/audit/advisory/organization in Bamako with a 2-10 employee company-size band. This is a platform signal, not an audited headcount. Source: https://www.linkedin.com/company/cabinet-cao/ (localized mirrors may appear in search).

## Design implication
The demo must not frame C.A.O as commercially weak. It should show how a compact, already competitive cabinet can **institutionalize and scale** what drives wins:
- qualify more opportunities without losing selectivity;
- retrieve the exact experience and proof required by a TDR;
- preserve expert/manager attention for high-judgment work;
- see offer deadlines, mission issues and cash milestones in one operating view;
- convert internal operating discipline into a later client-facing “pilotage augmenté” offer.

## Hypotheses to validate with the founder
Public information cannot prove internal workflow friction. The following remain hypotheses until interviews/data review: opportunity tracking method, evidence retrieval time, bid production effort, internal staffing bottlenecks, mission margin visibility, billing/collection cadence and actual systems used.
